This number is E − V + β₀ — the cycle rank of the graph only (its 1-skeleton), computed with the same rank–nullity idea from the lecture. It is not the full simplicial β₁: a graph alone has no 2-simplices to fill in, so unlike Module F's triangle, a cycle here can never become a boundary again — this count only ever grows. Building the actual Rips/Čech complex (with 2-simplices) is exactly what Lecture 9 and its lab do with GUDHI/Ripser. Drag slowly and notice there is no single ε where "the circle" cleanly appears.
