Digits: 1797 images in 64 dimensions, 60% for training. Network: three hidden layers of 256 ReLU units, Adam, no regularisation. “Real labels”: trained on the true labels. “Random labels”: trained on shuffled labels until the training accuracy is 100%, so the network memorises. “Untrained”: the random initial weights. Three runs with different initialisations. The pattern follows Ansuini et al. (NeurIPS 2019), but this is a small network: the pronounced early rise of their large networks is not reproduced.
