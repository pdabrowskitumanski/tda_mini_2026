Everything is computed in your browser: the optimal pairing is the exact minimum-cost assignment (Hungarian algorithm) for squared Euclidean cost, so W₂ is exact for these samples. The total variation uses a 2D histogram with the cell size you choose; MMD uses a Gaussian kernel; KL is the histogram estimate with the smoothing constant added to every cell. Samples are small (40 to 150 points), so every number has sampling noise: compare it with the grey floor.
