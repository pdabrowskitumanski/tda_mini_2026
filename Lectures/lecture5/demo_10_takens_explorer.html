Mutual information and autocorrelation

Everything is computed in your browser from a fresh simulation (Lorenz, Rössler) or an exact formula. The signal is standardised, then observational noise with the chosen percentage of its standard deviation is added. A random subset of delay vectors (1000, 1500 or 2500, your choice) is used for the two estimators, with brute-force neighbour search; with few points the dimension at large m is biased low (try the three settings at m = 8 for white noise). Dimension: the TwoNN estimator of Lecture 5 (maximum likelihood on the ratio of the first two neighbour distances). False neighbours: Kennel's criterion with ratio 10; neighbours closer than 200 samples in time are excluded. Delay choice: the first minimum of the mutual information (32 bins), and the first zero of the autocorrelation.
