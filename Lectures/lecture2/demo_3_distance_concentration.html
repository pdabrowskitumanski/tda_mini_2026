Asymptotic predictions (shown beside the readouts): cube — mean ≈ √(d/6), std ≈ 0.22; Gaussian — mean ≈ √(2d), std ≈ 0.87 (distances from a fixed query; between two random points the spreads are 0.24 and 1). The dashed line in the lower plot has slope −1/2, the predicted ∼ √(ln n / d) scaling. In “k-dim. data” mode the first k coordinates are uniform in [0,1] and every one of the d coordinates gets independent N(0, σ²) noise: with σ = 0 the ambient dimension changes nothing.
