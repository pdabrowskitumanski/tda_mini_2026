Radius r is the ball radius for all three complexes (Rips: an edge when the distance is at most 2r; ripser uses the edge length 2r). Čech uses the radius of the smallest enclosing ball of each triple; Alpha uses the Delaunay triangulation (triangle: circumradius; edge: half its length if the circle on it is empty, else the smaller circumradius of the triangles it belongs to). β₀ and β₁ are computed from the 2-skeleton over the field with two elements; β₁ does not depend on larger simplices. The intervals are exact (computed at the radii where a simplex enters).
